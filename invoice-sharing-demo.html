Invoice Sharing Demo

This page demonstrates the invoice sharing functionality implemented for PharmaSpot.

Invoice Sharing Demo

Features Implemented:

  • WhatsApp sharing (simulation mode)
  • SMS sharing (simulation mode)
  • Email sharing with PDF attachment
  • Template management system
  • Customer communication preferences
  • Communication tracking and analytics
  • PDF generation for invoices

API Endpoints Created:

  • /api/communication/* - Communication management
  • /api/templates/* - Template management
  • /api/notification-queue/* - Notification queue
  • /api/customer-preferences/* - Customer preferences
  • /api/invoice-sharing/* - Invoice sharing
